🏡 Fun Activities to Try Together at Home
🧦
Laundry Match-Up: Ask Liam to help match clean socks or sort spoons from forks during meal setup. This reinforces the exact 2-group categorization he excelled at in school!
⏱️
"My Turn, Your Turn" Game: While snacking or playing with blocks, practice passing an item with a friendly 5-count ("Mom's turn... 1, 2, 3... now Liam's turn!").
🌙
Visual 3-Minute Warning: Before turning off favorite shows or heading to bed, give a friendly 3-minute visual heads-up. This keeps Liam in his calm green zone during bedtime transitions.
